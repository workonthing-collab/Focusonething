Private programme

Srujan Fund Support is a privately operated startup support programme. It is not a government scheme. It is not run by, funded by, affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government ministry, department or agency, including Startup India, DPIIT, the Government of Gujarat, i-Hub Gujarat or its "Startup Srujan" scheme.

No guarantee of funding

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection or funding. Funding amounts, instruments and conditions are determined only after evaluation and are binding only when set out in a written funding agreement signed by both parties.

Introductions are not investment

Introductions to investors or ecosystem partners are a support service. They are not a commitment by the programme or by any third party to invest.

Information on this website

We try to keep information on this website accurate and current, but it may change without notice. The sample dashboard shown on the home page is an illustration and does not represent any real application.

Beware of fraud

We never ask for payment to process, approve or fast-track an application, and we communicate decisions only through the applicant dashboard and our official email. If anyone asks you for money in our name, do not pay. Report it to .
