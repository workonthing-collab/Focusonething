About us

About the programme

Srujan Fund Support provides seed funding and hands-on support to early-stage Indian startups that are solving real problems with innovative products and have a clear plan to grow.

Our objective is to help capable founders get from idea to a market-ready business by combining early capital with practical guidance, and to run a selection process that is structured, documented and fair.

How startups benefit

Early capital to validate and launch, plus guidance that strengthens the business and its readiness for future investment.

Seed funding, mentorship, financial and compliance guidance, and introductions to investors and ecosystem partners.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Your startup must meet all requirements marked Required. If you're unsure about any point, contact us before applying.

Registration Required

Registered in India as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) or Registered Partnership firm.

Incorporation Required

Incorporated not more than 5 years before the date of application.

Founder shareholding Required

Indian founders/promoters together hold at least 51% of the startup.

Innovation & scalability Required

Technology or innovation is core to the product, service or business model, with market fit, viable commercialisation and scope to scale.

DPIIT recognition Preferred

DPIIT-recognised startups are preferred, but recognition is not mandatory to apply.

Not eligible

Unregistered businesses and sole proprietorships; businesses that have already raised large institutional rounds; applications with false or misleading information; activities that are illegal or against public interest.

Evaluation process

Assessment framework

Every eligible application is assessed against the same six parameters, each scored from 1 to 5 by the reviewers assigned to it.

01

Team & Founder Capability

Relevant experience, commitment and the team's ability to execute the plan.

02

Problem Identification

A real, well-understood problem for a clearly defined customer.

03

Solution & Innovation

What is new about the approach and how defensible it is.

04

Market Opportunity

Size of the opportunity and how reachable it is for this team.

05

Business Model

A credible path to revenue and sustainable unit economics.

06

Traction & Execution

Evidence so far: users, pilots, revenue, LOIs or validated prototypes.

Scoring method

Each parameter is scored 1–5, for a maximum of 30. Each reviewer records a score, a recommendation and comments. Where more than one reviewer is assigned, their scores are considered together.

Review stages

Initial screening → document verification → evaluation by assigned reviewers → shortlisting and interaction → final decision. Each stage is shown on your dashboard.

Decision authority

Final decisions are taken by the programme management after considering reviewer scores and recommendations. Scores inform the decision but do not decide it automatically. Funding is confirmed only by a signed agreement.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is Srujan Fund Support a government scheme?

No. It is a privately operated programme and is not affiliated with any government ministry, department or scheme, including Startup India and the Government of Gujarat's "Startup Srujan" scheme run by i-Hub Gujarat.

Does the programme provide funding directly?

Yes. Selected startups receive seed funding directly from the programme, on terms set out in a signed funding agreement. Investor introductions are separate and are not a commitment to invest.

Is there an application fee?

There is no fee to apply.

How do I apply?

Contact the programme team with your pitch deck and company details. Once your application is recorded, log in to the portal with the same email address. We email you a secure login link, so no account or password is needed.

How do I track my application?

Log in to the applicant dashboard with the email you applied with. You'll see your status, remarks and any messages from the programme team.

What if my documents are incomplete?

Your status will change to "Documents Pending" with a remark explaining what's needed. You can update your document links from the portal.

How long does the review take?

It depends on the number of applications. Every status change is shown on your dashboard as it happens.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Only you, our administrators and the reviewers assigned to your application can see it, and it is used only to run the programme.

Can I edit my application after submitting?

Your application is maintained by the programme team. If documents are requested, you can update them from the portal. For other corrections, email us with your Application ID.
