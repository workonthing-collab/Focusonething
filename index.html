About the program

Srujan Fund Support is a privately run startup support program. We look for founders solving real problems with a clear plan, and we help them get from idea to traction with capital, guidance and structure.

Founder-first

We judge the team, the problem and the plan, not just how polished the deck is.

Track everything online

Log in with the email you applied with and see your status, messages and funding updates.

Clear decisions

Every application gets reviewed and receives a decision, with status visible at each stage.

Who can apply

Check these before you start. If you're unsure about any point, write to us before applying.

You're eligible if

  • Your startup is registered in India as a Private Limited Company, LLP or Registered Partnership
  • It was incorporated not more than 5 years before the date of application
  • You have a business idea or product with market fit, viable commercialisation and scope for scaling
  • Technology or innovation is a core part of your product, service or business model
  • Founders (Indian promoters) hold at least 51% of the company

Not eligible

  • Unregistered businesses or sole proprietorships
  • Businesses that have already raised large institutional rounds
  • Applications with incomplete, false or misleading information
  • Activities that are illegal or against public interest

DPIIT-recognised startups are preferred but recognition is not mandatory.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an application fee?

No. Applying through this portal is free.

How do I see my application status?

Create a login on this portal using the same email you applied with, verify your email, and your application status will appear on your dashboard. If nothing shows, email us.

How long does the review take?

Timelines depend on the number of applications. You'll see every status change on your dashboard: submitted, under review, shortlisted, and the final decision.

Is Srujan Fund Support a government scheme?

No. Srujan Fund Support is a privately run program and is not affiliated with any government scheme or department.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your application and documents are visible only to you and our review team, and are used only to evaluate your application.

Can I change my application details?

Your details are managed by our team. If anything needs correcting, email us with your application ID.
