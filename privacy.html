Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Srujan Fund Support ("we") collects through srujanfundsupport.in, why, and how it is protected.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password when you create a login. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Application details: information about your startup that you send us when applying (company, founders, contact details, sector, funding requirement and documents), which our team records in the portal.
  • Program records: your application status, messages from our team, and funding or tranche details if you are selected.
  • Review notes: internal scores and notes from our reviewers. These are not shown to applicants.

How we use it

Only to evaluate your application, communicate with you, show you your application status, and administer any support provided. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

You can see your own record after verifying your email. Our administrators can see all records. Reviewers can see only the applications assigned to them. We may share information with professional advisers or authorities only where legally required.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region – asia-south1). Access is restricted by security rules so that each person sees only what they are permitted to see.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's storage only to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Retention

We keep application records for as long as needed to run the program and meet legal obligations, after which they are deleted.

Your rights
