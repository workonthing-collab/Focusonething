Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Srujan Fund Support ("we", "the programme") collects through srujanfundsupport.in, why we collect it, and how it is protected.

Information we collect

  • Login details: your name and email address from your Google account when you sign in with Google. Your Google password is entered only on Google's own page; we never see or store it.
  • Application details: information you submit in the online application, including company details, founder and team details, contact numbers, your pitch, funding requirement and links to your documents.
  • Programme records: your Application ID, application status, remarks and messages from the programme team, and funding details if you are selected.
  • Review records: reviewer scores, recommendations and internal notes, and an audit log of status changes. Scores and internal notes are not shown to applicants.

How we use it

Only to verify your identity, evaluate your application, communicate with you, show you your application status, and administer any funding or support provided. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

You can see your own application after verifying your email. Programme administrators can see all applications. Reviewers can see only the applications assigned to them. We may share information with professional advisers, or with authorities where required by law.

Documents

Documents are submitted as links to files you host (for example on Google Drive). You control those files and can revoke access at any time; please keep them available until a decision is made.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region, asia-south1). Access is restricted by security rules so that each person sees only what they are permitted to see.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember which notifications you have seen. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Retention

We keep application records for as long as needed to run the programme and meet legal obligations, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, by writing to . We will respond within a reasonable time.

Grievances

For privacy questions or grievances, email with the subject "Privacy".
